# Fathom

**Dive into your media.** An all-in-one client for Jellyfin, on desktop and
Android: your movies, shows, music and Live TV, Seerr requests, and YouTube,
without leaving the app.

Fathom connects to the Jellyfin server you already run. It plays everything
through mpv, so you get direct play, hardware decoding, and real subtitle and
audio track control, and it covers most of Jellyfin's server management, so
you rarely need the web dashboard. It runs on Linux (AppImage), Windows, and
Android phones and tablets, with Android TV in progress.

## How it differs from the Trace apps

- **Nothing to install on a server.** Fathom is an app that connects to your
  Jellyfin server (and, if you use it, Seerr). The Trace apps' Docker,
  environment and backup pages don't apply.
- **Your Jellyfin account.** You sign in with Jellyfin, Quick Connect
  included. There's no separate Fathom account or sign-in setup.
- **No Trace AI, no sync with the Trace apps.** It doesn't share data with
  them.
